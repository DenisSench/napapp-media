A 3 AM wake-up can stay calm and short 🦉✨
First, wait a few minutes: many babies settle back on their own. If you go in, keep the lights low and your voice soft, and skip the play. Feed or change baby only if they need it, then lay them straight back down so they learn night-time is for sleeping. Safe sleep: on their back, on a firm, flat surface, in an empty crib, every sleep until age 1. Save this and follow for more tips. Napapp logs sleep and feeds in one tap.
