POV: the night feed turned into playtime 🔅🌙
At night: lights down low, little talking and a quiet voice, no play, and change them only if they need it (NHS).
Keeping the mood calm and quiet helps babies learn nighttime is for sleep (Cleveland Clinic). By day, open the curtains and play.
Safe sleep: on their back, on a firm, flat surface, in an empty crib, every sleep until age 1.
