Formula feeding? A partner can share night feeds (NHS). Breastfeeding? They can take the early morning change and dressing, or once feeding is settled, occasionally give expressed milk at night.
Take shifts with changes, bottle feeds and rocking (AAP), and when you're off duty, rest can matter more than chores (NHS).
Safe sleep: on their back, on a firm, flat surface, in an empty crib, every sleep until age 1.
