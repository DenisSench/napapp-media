6 PM and the evening shift clocks in. A daily fussy stretch, often between 6 p.m. and midnight, is common in young babies, and it usually eases over the first few months.
What many parents try: dim lights, a quieter house, gentle motion in a carrier or their arms. It won't fix every evening, and that's okay. Every baby is different, so check with your pediatrician if the crying is new, intense or worrying. Napapp: link on our Page.
