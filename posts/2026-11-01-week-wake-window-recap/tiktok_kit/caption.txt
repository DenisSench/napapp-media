0-12 months: how long between naps?
Every typical wake window from 0-12 months, in one place: 0-1 month 30-60 min, 1-3 months 1-2 hours, 3-4 months 1¼-2½ hours, 5-7 months 2-4 hours, 7-10 months 2½-4½ hours, 10-12 months 3-6 hours. These are typical ranges, not targets, and every baby is different. Source: Cleveland Clinic. If your clocks changed this week, sleepy cues are a good guide while things settle.
