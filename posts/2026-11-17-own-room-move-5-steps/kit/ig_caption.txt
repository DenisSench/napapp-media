Moving baby into their own room? Here's a calm way to do it 🏡🛏️
The AAP and NHS both say to keep your baby's crib in your room for at least the first 6 months, so plan the move for after that. Set the new crib up for safe sleep: a firm, flat mattress with a fitted sheet only, and nothing else in it. Then keep everything else familiar. The NHS suggests a simple, soothing bedtime routine like a bath, fresh diaper and pajamas, a story and dim lights, and Cleveland Clinic says to keep nap and night sleep times as consistent as you can. From about 4 months, the AAP suggests putting babies down drowsy but not yet asleep, which helps them learn to fall asleep on their own, in their own bed.
