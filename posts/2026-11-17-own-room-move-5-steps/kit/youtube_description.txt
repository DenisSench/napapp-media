Moving baby into their own room? Here's a calm way to do it 🏡🛏️
Room-share for at least the first 6 months, then set the new crib up with a fitted sheet only.
Keep the bedtime routine and bedtime they know, and from about 4 months put them down drowsy but awake.
Safe sleep: on their back, on a firm, flat surface, in an empty crib, every sleep until age 1.
