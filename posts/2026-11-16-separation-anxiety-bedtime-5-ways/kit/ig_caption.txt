Clingy at bedtime is a stage, and it passes 🫂🌙
The AAP says separation anxiety is a normal part of development that often starts in the second half of the first year and usually fades around the second birthday. Their tips: practise short separations during the day (tell them "I'll be right back"), play peek-a-boo so they learn you always come back, and keep a loving, consistent bedtime routine. Say goodnight in a calm, matter-of-fact way, tell them "I'll be right back," then leave, and come back when you say you will. Cleveland Clinic adds that a quick goodbye ritual your child knows makes separations easier, so use the same words every night. Until this stage fades, the AAP says your child may need reassurance several times, night after night.
