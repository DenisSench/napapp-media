Night weaning goes best gently, at your baby's pace 🍼✨
From 6 months, some babies need fewer night feeds. Offer full feeds in the day and drop one feed at a time.
Keep night feeds dim, quiet and calm, and follow their cues: if they're truly hungry, feed them.
Safe sleep: on their back, on a firm, flat surface, in an empty crib, every sleep until age 1.
