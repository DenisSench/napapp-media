18-24 months and fighting the second nap?
Moving from two naps to one usually happens somewhere around 18-24 months, per Cleveland Clinic, and it's often a messy, back-and-forth stretch. That's normal. Total sleep matters too: toddlers 1-2 years typically need 11-14 hours across the day and night (AASM). These are typical ranges, not targets, and every toddler is different.
