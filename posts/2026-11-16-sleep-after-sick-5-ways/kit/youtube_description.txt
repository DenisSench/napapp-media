A gentle way back to routine once your little one is feeling better 💛🧸
Extra daytime cuddles are fine. Restart your usual bedtime routine and regular bedtime and wake-up.
Night wakes: keep it dim, quiet and comfort briefly. Steady steps, night after night.
Safe sleep: on their back, on a firm, flat surface, in an empty crib, every sleep until age 1.
