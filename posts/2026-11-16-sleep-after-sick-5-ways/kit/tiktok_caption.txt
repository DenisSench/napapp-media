Sick weeks can throw any routine off. Here's a gentle way back 💛🧸 Once your little one is feeling better, extra daytime cuddles are fine. Then lean on what's familiar: the NHS suggests a simple, soothing bedtime routine like a bath, fresh nappy and pajamas, a story and dim lights, and Cleveland Clinic says a regular sleep-wake schedule helps babies fall asleep and stay asleep. If they wake at night, keep it calm and quiet: the AAP says you can comfort them when needed, with lights low. Safe-sleep basics stay the same: back to sleep on their back, on a firm, flat surface, in an empty crib, for every sleep until age 1. What helps your family get back on track?
