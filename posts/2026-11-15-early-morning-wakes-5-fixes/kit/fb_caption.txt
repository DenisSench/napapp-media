Run through these 🐓☕ Cleveland Clinic says it's natural and common for babies to wake during the night, and the NHS says young babies wake because they need to be fed (and hunger can still wake some babies at 6 to 12 months). Being too hot or too cold can disturb sleep too (NHS); Cleveland Clinic suggests a comfortable room, generally 68 to 72°F (20 to 22°C). Help baby's internal clock with bright, sunny spaces by day and no bright lights at night, keep naps in the day (an overtired baby can have a harder time falling and staying asleep), and try a 20- to 30-minute bedtime routine (Cleveland Clinic).
