Travelling with baby? 6 safe-sleep tips for the trip ✈️🧳
Pack a routine that works anywhere, and bring or book a portable crib with no broken or missing parts.
Fitted sheet only inside, crib away from cords, lamps and TVs, and if they fall asleep in the car seat, move them onto their back in the crib once you arrive.
Safe sleep: on their back, on a firm, flat surface, in an empty crib, every sleep until age 1.
