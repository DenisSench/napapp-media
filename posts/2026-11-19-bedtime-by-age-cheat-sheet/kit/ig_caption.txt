How much sleep does your baby need? 📋⭐
The American Academy of Sleep Medicine recommends 12 to 16 hours per 24 hours for babies 4 to 12 months, 11 to 14 hours for 1 to 2 years and 10 to 13 hours for 3 to 5 years, naps included, and the AAP supports those guidelines. Under 4 months, the AASM sets no target because normal newborn sleep varies so widely, and the AAP notes newborns may only sleep 1 or 2 hours at a time. Naps count, so add up day and night sleep across 24 hours. To set a bedtime, Cleveland Clinic suggests starting with when your child needs to wake up and counting backward. Then keep the day regular: the AAP says the same waking time, nap time and routine help with a smooth bedtime. Back to sleep on their back, on a firm, flat surface, in an empty crib, for every sleep until age 1. Which age range is your little one in?
