How much sleep does your baby need? 📋⭐
The AASM recommends, per 24 hours with naps: 12-16 hours at 4-12 months, 11-14 at 1-2 years, 10-13 at 3-5 years.
Naps count toward the total. To set bedtime, start from wake-up time and count backward.
Safe sleep: on their back, on a firm, flat surface, in an empty crib, every sleep until age 1.
