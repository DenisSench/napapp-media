How much sleep, 4 months to 2 years?
Typical total sleep in 24 hours, naps included: 4-12 months 12-16 hours, 1-2 years 11-14 hours (AASM guidance, endorsed by the AAP). There's no official range under 4 months, because newborn sleep varies so much (Cleveland Clinic). These are typical ranges, not targets, and every baby is different.
