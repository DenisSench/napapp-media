Hungry or just tired? How to read the cues 🍼💤
Early hunger cues (AAP): licking lips, rooting, tongue out, hands to mouth. Crying is a late sign of hunger.
Sleepy cues (Cleveland Clinic): yawning, droopy eyelids, rubbing eyes, staring off. Fussing but refusing a feed can mean tired, not hungry.
Safe sleep: on their back, on a firm, flat surface, in an empty crib, every sleep until age 1.
