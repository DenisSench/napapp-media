8-10 months: crawling in the crib at 2am?
Crawling at 2am, crying when you leave the room, naps all over the place: the "8-10 month regression" is really a stretch of big development, not something you caused. "Regression" is just the common name, not a diagnosis, and there's no guaranteed fix. Every baby moves through it differently.
