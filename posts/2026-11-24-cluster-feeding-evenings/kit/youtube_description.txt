Evening cluster feeds? 4 ways through 🌆☕
Cluster feeding is very normal in the first 3 to 4 months, and prolactin is usually lowest in the evening, so feeds often bunch up then.
Feed on cues, watch bottle-fed babies for break cues, hand dinner and older kids to a partner, and rest when baby sleeps.
Safe sleep: on their back, on a firm, flat surface, in an empty crib, every sleep until age 1.
