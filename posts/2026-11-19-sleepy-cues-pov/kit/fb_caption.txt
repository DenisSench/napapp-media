Catch the sleepy cues and naps get easier to start 🥱👀 Watch for yawns, droopy eyelids, eye rubbing, ear pulling, staring off, or turning away from toys and lights. When you spot one, lay baby down drowsy but still awake, before they get overtired, because an overtired baby can find it harder to settle.
