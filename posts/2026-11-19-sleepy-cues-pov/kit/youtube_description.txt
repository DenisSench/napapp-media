Catch the sleepy cues and naps get easier to start 🥱👀
Watch for yawns, droopy eyelids, eye rubbing, ear pulling, staring off, or turning away from toys and lights.
Spot one? Lay baby down drowsy but still awake, before they get overtired.
Safe sleep: on their back, on a firm, flat surface, in an empty crib, every sleep until age 1.
