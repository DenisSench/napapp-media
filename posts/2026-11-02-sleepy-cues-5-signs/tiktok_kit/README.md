Yawning or meltdown? Early vs late sleepy cues by age.
Early cues are quieter: yawning, staring off and frowning in newborns; eye rubbing, ear pulling and losing interest in play in older babies; yawning and slowing down in toddlers. Late cues get louder: frantic crying, clinginess, or a toddler who suddenly seems wired. Once little ones are overtired, settling can get harder, so many parents watch for the early ones.
