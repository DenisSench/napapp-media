Yawning or meltdown? Early vs late sleepy cues by age.
Early cues are quieter: yawning, staring off and frowning in newborns; eye rubbing, ear pulling and losing interest in play in older babies; yawning and slowing down in toddlers. Late cues get louder: frantic crying, clinginess, or a toddler who suddenly seems wired. Once little ones are overtired, settling can get harder, so many parents watch for the early ones. Every baby is different. Save this cheat sheet 🤍 Log naps in one tap to see when cues tend to show up. Napapp, link in bio.
Source: Cleveland Clinic. Not medical advice. Check with your pediatrician about your baby's sleep.
