Yawning or meltdown? Early vs late sleepy cues by age.
Early cues are quieter (yawning, staring off, eye rubbing, losing interest in play); late cues get louder (frantic crying, clinginess, a toddler who suddenly seems wired). Once little ones are overtired, settling can get harder. Every baby is different.
