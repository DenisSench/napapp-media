4-month naps, a little easier 😴⏱️
Cleveland Clinic puts typical awake time at 3 to 4 months at about 1.25 to 2.5 hours, and says to watch for early sleepy cues like yawning and eye rubbing. Keep a short nap routine that's the same each time, dim the room, and, as the AAP suggests from about 4 months, lay baby down drowsy but still awake.
