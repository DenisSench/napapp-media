Feeds and naps by age: typical ranges, not a schedule 📋🌼
Newborns: breastfed babies often feed 10 to 12 times in 24 hours, and bottle-fed newborns usually every 2 to 3 hours (AAP). Sleep comes in short stretches.
By 4 months, naps often add up to 3 to 5 hours a day; from 4 to 12 months, one, two or three naps can all be fine; from 1 to 2 years, likely one nap.
Safe sleep: on their back, on a firm, flat surface, in an empty crib, every sleep until age 1.
