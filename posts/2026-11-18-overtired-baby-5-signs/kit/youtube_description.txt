Overtired or just sleepy? 5 signs to spot 🥱🌀
Louder, more frantic crying, seeming wired, fighting sleep, extra clinginess, and more fussiness than usual.
What helps next time: put them down at the first sleepy cues, like eye rubbing or droopy eyelids.
Safe sleep: on their back, on a firm, flat surface, in an empty crib, every sleep until age 1.
