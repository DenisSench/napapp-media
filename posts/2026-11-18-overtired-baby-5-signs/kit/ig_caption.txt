Sleepy and overtired can look different, and spotting the difference helps 🥱🌀
Cleveland Clinic says overtired babies often cry louder and more frantically than usual. When babies get too tired, a rush of cortisol and adrenaline can amp them up instead of calming them down, so they're harder to get down. Other signs to watch for: extra clinginess, and more fussiness and irritability than usual. What helps next time: Cleveland Clinic suggests putting them down as soon as you notice drowsiness, like eye rubbing and droopy eyelids, and keeping a regular sleep-wake schedule so they're less likely to get overtired.
