Easier naps start before the nap 😴📖
Watch for sleepy cues like yawns and eye rubs, then swap busy play for a quiet book.
Dim the lights, sing softly, and from about 4 months put baby down drowsy but not yet asleep.
Safe sleep: on their back, on a firm, flat surface, in an empty crib, every sleep until age 1.
