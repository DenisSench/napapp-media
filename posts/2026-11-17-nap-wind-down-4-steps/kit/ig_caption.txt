Easier naps start before the nap 😴📖
Cleveland Clinic suggests putting baby down as soon as you notice sleepy cues like yawning, eye rubbing or droopy eyelids, and swapping energetic play or screens for a book or a soft song in a dim room. A short routine helps baby know sleep is coming, and the AAP suggests that from about 4 months, babies go down drowsy but not yet asleep. Back to sleep on their back, on a firm, flat surface, in an empty crib, for every sleep until age 1. Which sleepy cue does yours show first?
