Short naps? Here are 5 steps toward better naps 💤🌤️
Know your baby's wake window, and put them down at the first sleepy cues like yawning or eye rubbing.
Dim the lights, keep nap times steady, and from about 4 months put them down drowsy but not asleep.
Safe sleep: on their back, on a firm, flat surface, in an empty crib, every sleep until age 1.
