Night wake? Here's a calm order to try 🌛⭐
From about 4 months, wait a few minutes first: the AAP says it's normal for babies to wake and drift back.
Still crying? Check all's OK, pat and reassure in the crib, try not to pick them up, and leave while calm but awake.
You can always attend if baby needs a feed, a clean diaper or comfort. Safe sleep: on their back, on a firm, flat surface, in an empty crib, every sleep until age 1.
