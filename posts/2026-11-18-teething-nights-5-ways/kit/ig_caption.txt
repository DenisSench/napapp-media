Teething nights can be tough on everyone. Here are 5 gentle ways to comfort your baby 🦷🫶
The NHS notes that teething discomfort can wake some babies at night. For the wind-down, the AAP suggests a comforting bath, a bedtime massage and a little white noise on a low setting that won't harm their hearing. If they wake, wash your hands and gently rub their gums with a clean fingertip: the AAP says light, focused pressure on the gums can often soothe them back to sleep. In the day, soft rubber teething toys can soothe aching gums, and toys stay out of the crib at sleep time. The AAP calls teething a natural milestone.
