Teething nights? 5 gentle ways to comfort your baby 🦷🫶
Wind down with a comforting bath, a gentle bedtime massage and a little white noise set low.
If they wake, wash your hands and gently rub their gums. In the day, offer soft teething toys, never in the crib.
Safe sleep: on their back, on a firm, flat surface, in an empty crib, every sleep until age 1.
