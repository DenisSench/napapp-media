Short naps don't always mean a rough night.
Short naps don't automatically mean a rough night. Young babies' sleep cycles are short (typically about 45-60 minutes, per NHS guidance), and nights depend on lots of things. Looking at total daytime sleep tells you more than one nap does. Cleveland Clinic says around 4 months that's usually 3-5 hours, as a typical range, not a target. Every baby is different.
