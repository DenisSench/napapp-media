Suddenly feeding around the clock? 🌻🍼
A growth spurt can make a baby seem hungry all the time (AAP), and feeding more often, sometimes constantly, is very normal (NHS).
Breastfeeding: feed as often and as long as they want. Bottle feeding: watch for break cues like turning away. And let someone bring you dinner.
Safe sleep: on their back, on a firm, flat surface, in an empty crib, every sleep until age 1.
