Still waking at night? Here's what's common at each age, so save this for the next 3am.
Newborns wake often, as often as every 1-2 hours, and waking repeatedly through the night is expected for the first few months. From 3 to 6 months, some babies sleep 5 to 8 hours or longer at night, but not all. Around 6 months, it's normal to wake and settle back after a few minutes. Later in the first year, teething, hunger or separation anxiety can wake some babies, and night waking phases can come and go in the first few years. Separation anxiety usually fades around the second birthday.
