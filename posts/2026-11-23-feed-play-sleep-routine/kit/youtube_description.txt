Eat, play, sleep: 6 tips to start a gentle daytime rhythm 🍼🧸
Offer a feed when baby wakes, following hunger cues rather than the clock, then play and supervised tummy time, then sleep on their back.
Cleveland Clinic suggests feeding at the start of the bedtime routine so baby doesn't rely on feeding to fall asleep; from 4 months, try putting baby down drowsy. In the early weeks, dozing during feeds is common, so keep feeding until they've finished.
Safe sleep: on their back, on a firm, flat surface, in an empty crib, every sleep until age 1.
