Caption

The real reason your baby's naps are only 30 minutes.
A young baby's sleep cycle is typically about 45-60 minutes (typical range, per NHS), and they sleep lightly near the end of it, so waking after one cycle is really common.
Timing, light, noise or being overtired can play a part too, and many babies' naps get longer with age. These are possibilities, not a diagnosis, and there's no guaranteed fix. Every baby is different.
