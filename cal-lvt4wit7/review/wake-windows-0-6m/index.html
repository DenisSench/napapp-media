Is your baby awake too long? Here are typical wake windows from birth to 7 months 🌙
Wake windows are the stretches your baby is awake between sleeps, and they get longer as babies grow.
These are typical ranges, not a schedule to hit. Every baby is different, and sleepy cues (yawning, staring off, rubbing eyes) count for more than the clock.
