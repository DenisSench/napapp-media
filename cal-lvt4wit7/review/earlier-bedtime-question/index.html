Later bedtime, later wake-up? Often not.
It's one of the most common things parents try, and it often doesn't work that way. Cleveland Clinic notes overtired babies can have a harder time falling and staying asleep.
Some parents test a slightly earlier bedtime for a few days and watch. It's one option, not a rule, and there are no guarantees. Every baby is different.
