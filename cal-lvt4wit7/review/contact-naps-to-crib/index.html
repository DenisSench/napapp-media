Caption

Contact naps every day? One gentle first step.
Contact naps are sweet, common, and nothing to feel guilty about. When you want some crib naps too, many parents start with one nap a day and a short, consistent routine. Every crib nap follows AAP safe sleep: baby on their back, on a firm flat surface, in an empty crib. And if you start to feel sleepy while holding your baby, move them to their crib. Every baby is different, and it can take time. Save this for later 🤍 Log naps in one tap to see how it's going. Napapp, link in bio.
Not medical advice. Check with your pediatrician about naps and safe sleep.
